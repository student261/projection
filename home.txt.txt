FAQ
Label: COMMON QUESTIONS
What is interactive engagement?
 Interactive engagement covers games and activities that invite people to move, play or take part, usually on a floor, wall or similar surface.
What is the difference between interactive engagement and interactive spaces?
 Interactive spaces describes the physical surface, such as a floor or wall. Interactive engagement describes the game or activity running on that surface.
Can games be branded for a specific campaign?
 Yes, game content, visuals and scoring can be built around a brand, product or theme.
How many people can play at once?
 This depends on the surface size and the tracking setup, but several formats support group play.
What affects the cost of an interactive game?
 Cost depends on the surface size, the tracking method, the complexity of the game content and the length of the activation.
Can the same setup run different games over time?
 Game content can be swapped for new campaigns or seasons, depending on the system installed.
How do we get started?
 Share your space, your audience and the kind of activity you want people to play, and we will recommend a suitable format.
