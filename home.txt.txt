2. The Challenge
Label: THE CHALLENGE
 H2: The Challenge in Modern Retail
01. Declining Foot Traffic
 Static windows and displays don't hold attention the way they used to.
02. Short Dwell Times
 Shoppers move through stores quickly, with limited time spent engaging with any one brand.
03. E-commerce Competition
 Physical stores struggle to match the convenience and personalization of online shopping.
04. Static Merchandising
 Traditional displays rarely update or invite any kind of interaction.
05. Lack of Analytics
 Physical retail often has little visibility into how people actually move through and engage with a space.
06. Brand Differentiation
 Standing out in a crowded retail environment, mall or high street is harder than ever.

11. FAQ
Label: COMMON QUESTIONS
Can a shop window be interactive?
 Yes, where the window and lighting suit the setup. Content can be projected or displayed so it reacts as people walk past.
What can shoppers do at an interactive retail display?
 They can play a short game, explore products, see large format visuals, or interact with an AI guide, depending on the setup.
Does the store need major changes?
 Requirements depend on the space and the experience. Surface, lighting, ceiling height and equipment placement are reviewed during planning.
Can it work near windows with strong sunlight?
 It depends on the equipment and lighting conditions. This is reviewed during planning.
Can the content change for different campaigns?
 Yes, content can be planned to change with new products, sales and seasons.
Can it be customized to our brand?
 Visuals and activities can be planned around your brand, products and space.
How do we get started?
 Share your store, your audience and what you want shoppers to do. We will discuss a suitable approach for your project.
