13. FAQ
What is an interactive learning space?
 It uses projection, motion tracking and interactive software to let students explore and interact with digital content, rather than just view it.
Can interactive projection be used in a regular classroom?
 Yes. It can be set up on suitable floors, walls or other surfaces, depending on the room's layout and lighting.
Can the content be customized for our curriculum?
 Yes. Activities and content are built around the subject, age group and learning goals you specify.
How many students can use the experience at once?
 This depends on the space and setup — systems can be designed for individual or group interaction.
Which spaces work best for this?
 Classrooms, STEM labs, libraries, auditoriums and activity areas are all common setups.
Does installation require major changes to the room?
 Most installations work with existing floors, walls and ceilings, with requirements reviewed during planning.
Is internet access required for daily use?
 No. Core activities run locally; internet is only needed for content updates.
How long does installation take?
 Timeline depends on room size and setup — this is confirmed during the planning stage.
Do you provide training for teachers?
 Yes, training is included as part of installation and support.
How do we get started?
 Share your space, goals and the kind of learning experience you want to create, and we'll recommend the right approach for your school.
